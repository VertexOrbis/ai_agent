With a hands-on approach to technology, he spends much of his time exploring new AI models, cloud platforms, automation tools and emerging digital technologies. About VertexOrbis

An independent, founder-led studio.

VertexOrbis Technologies is a small, independent technology studio based in Taiwan, focused on practical applications of artificial intelligence, cloud computing and automation.

Because the studio is intentionally small, there is no account layer, no hand-off and no sales process — inquiries are handled directly by the person who designs and builds the system. Work starts from a working prototype rather than a slide deck, and scope stays narrow until something actually runs.

The goal is simple: turn new technology into useful systems people can actually use.
